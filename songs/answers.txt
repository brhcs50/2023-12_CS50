Hypothesize about why the way you’ve calculated this aura might not be very representative of the listener. What better ways of calculating this aura would you propose?

Spotify recommends new songs based on what you've already been listening to. This creates a kind of feedback loop, where you are only being exposed to something
you are already familiar with. So once the algorithm has decided "who you are" you won't be exposed to other styles of music. Spotify says you're a happy person
because they only play dance music for you while you are in the shower and can't change it.

A different method may be to only include music that the person has specifically selected, or is from one of their manually created playlists.
