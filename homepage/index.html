How much should a person reveal of himself to the prying eyes of the public; to the soulless community of internet gawkers? Surely, an adult man has the wherewithall and wisdom gained from a lifetime of online experience to reject the wanton sharing of all things personal for the mere reward of anonymous approval.

Yet here he stands, against the wall, in a situation of his own making, forced to expose his innermost secrets and desires for the amusement of those who will judge him.

So be it.

Within these few pages you shall find all the information you will need to break passwords, locate personal accounts, and shatter my anonymity. I am at your mercy.
