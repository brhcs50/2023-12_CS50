I started playing this online computer game about a year after it released, in early spring 2006. I've played it off and on over the years. It generally has a new expansion released every 2-3 years, and I will come back to play for a little bit and see what the new features are. Over the years a lot of the original game world has been changed, and the gameplay is almost entirely different.

But in August of 2019, the original game was re-released as "World of Warcraft Classic". This was the game as it released in 2004, with no expansion features, or changes. Since I had missed out on the first year of the game, this was very appealing to me as a nostalgic way to relive that time, and see what I could have accomplished if my gameplay had been on the level it is now.

This re-release has now also changed, having been overwritten with the early expansions released since 2019. Fortunately, the original game has been granted its own servers as a permanent "era" of classic game. This has become the version of the game I come back to every few months, and still enjoy playing.
